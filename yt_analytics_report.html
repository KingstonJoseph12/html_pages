Why the aggregate drop is bigger than the mean drop

Five Changes.
Each Tied to Data.

Every recommendation below is traceable to a specific number from the analysis. No generic YouTube advice. Each has a falsifiable success metric for 14-day validation.

1
UPLOAD AUTOMATION CONFIG

The 48-upload week had the lowest POST median views (954) vs 30-upload week at highest mean (1,521). Within-POST weekly frequency vs mean views: r=−0.9998 (n=3). Keep only 3 of the 6 daily slots — prioritize 17:00, 19:00, 21:00 IST.

Cap rendered clip duration at 14 seconds

Evidence: Duration vs retention r=−0.608 (jobs.db, n=43). 10–14s bucket: 109.8% mean retention. 15–19s bucket: 92.5% mean retention. PRE had 226 videos in 10–14s bucket; POST only has 36.
🎯 Success: Median avg % viewed rises from 89.72% to >100% within 14 days

Break the template: ban stem repeats, allow 5 hashtags, add descriptive text
